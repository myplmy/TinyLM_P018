WHAT TO RECORD
  1. per model: storage MB, tensor-sum resident MB, RSS delta, latent vs dequant split
  2. the TRANSFER RATE line (how much of the storage reduction became resident)
  3. whether the storage order and the resident order INVERTED

HOW TO ACT ON IT
  transfer near 100 percent -> our reporting was right. Close P034 early.
  transfer near 0, tying-only gain (expected) -> the ternary/sparse gain is
      THEORETICAL so far. Then:
        a. report packed_mb AND runtime_mb everywhere, never one alone
        b. P034 stage 2 (drop the latent weight after freeze) is the cheapest
           real win - inference needs no backward pass, so latent is dead weight
        c. stage 3 (int8 storage) and stage 4 (5-bit packing) follow
  resident WORSE than storage predicts -> an unexpected copy exists. Find it first.

  Note stage 2 alone should roughly HALVE resident memory. That is the largest
  single lever available today and it needs no custom kernel.

LIMITS: RSS includes the python/torch runtime (read the DELTA only) / tensor sum
  excludes activations and KV cache / this measures TODAY's implementation, which
  is exactly the baseline stages 2-4 have to beat.
